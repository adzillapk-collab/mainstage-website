## 2. The problem

**Headline:** Most reels look busy. Very few hold attention.

You've probably been here:

- **The edit is "fine," but nobody watches past second three.** The hook was buried, so the scroll won.
- **Random zooms and emojis everywhere.** It looks busy, but nothing on screen actually helps your point land.
- **You wrote a brief and got something else back.** It's hard to describe visuals in a doc, and harder for an editor to guess what you meant.
- **Round five of revisions.** Every change means re-doing work, so one reel drags on for a week.

**Closing line:**
The problem isn't effort. It's judgment: knowing what to cut, when to move, and what to show.

## 3. What makes Main Stage different

**Headline:** AI can cut a video. It can't tell why one works.

We use AI for the repetitive parts, like rough cuts and captions. That frees our time for the parts that decide whether people keep watching:

## 4. How it works

**Headline:** From raw footage to finished reel, step by step.

**Sub:** No guesswork, no endless back-and-forth. You see the plan before we build it.

**1. Send your footage**
Upload your raw clips and fill in a short brief: what the reel is about, who it's for, and any style you like.

**2. We build the cut**
We shape the story, tighten the pacing and sharpen the hook. This is where most of the craft goes.

**3. You see the plan on your own video**
Before any animation, we place rough visual frames right on your cut, showing what goes where and when. You watch it, then approve it or leave comments on [Frame.io]. No imagining visuals from a written doc.

**4. We bring it to life**
Once you approve, we animate. That covers full-screen graphics, elements layered behind or over you, and smooth camera moves that keep the frame alive. Captions and sound go in too.

**5. Final polish**
You review the finished reel. If anything needs a small tweak, we fix it. There are no surprises, because you already signed off on the plan.

**6. Delivered, ready to post**
Your finished reel lands fast, exported for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn.

## 11. FAQ

**Headline:** Questions? Answered.

**What kind of videos do you edit?**
Talking-head reels, meaning you speaking to the camera, up to 90 seconds. Podcast clips work too, as long as the focus is on the person speaking.

**Why do I review a plan before you animate?**
Because describing visuals in words rarely works. Seeing rough frames on your own video makes it instantly clear. You approve the direction early, so the final reel matches what you had in mind.

**What if I don't like the final reel?**
The final review is for small fixes, like timing, a word in a caption or a colour. The big decisions are made at the plan stage, so final changes stay quick.

**Do you use AI?**
Yes, for the repetitive parts, like a first rough cut and caption transcription. Every decision that matters, from the hook and pacing to the story and visuals, is made by a person.

**How do I send footage?**
Share your files through our upload form. Any camera works, phone footage included.

**Can you match my brand?**
Yes. Send us your fonts, colours and a few reels you like. We'll keep every edit consistent.

**Do I need to sign a long contract?**
No. Packs run month to month, and you can stop whenever you like.

**Is my footage safe?**
Your files stay private, are only used for your reels, and are never shared.
